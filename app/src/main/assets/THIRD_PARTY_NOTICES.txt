Pocket AI — third-party software notices

The application bundles CPU inference code, multilingual search, dedicated
English-to-Urdu/Punjabi translation, a small English language model, and a
quantized multilingual Whisper tiny speech model. Exact model provenance,
licenses and limitations are in OFFLINE_MODEL_NOTICES.txt and the pinned
offline-model-manifest.json. Android TTS voice data is not bundled.
